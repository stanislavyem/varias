You are building a full-stack web app called “Risk App” for insurance risk assessment and action management. Use Next.js (TypeScript) + Tailwind for UI, Prisma ORM, and PostgreSQL for the database. Implement role-based access control with 3 roles: CARRIER, AGENT, INSURED. Authentication can be email/password (simple) and roles stored on the user record.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT change any scoring metrics, rating bands, weights, or scoring math. Scoring must match the Excel workbook “Risk_App_Scoring_Engine_Clean_NoLinkedSources.xlsx” exactly:
- Questions table comes from sheet “Questions” with columns: QuestionID, Pillar, Topic, Question Text, RespType, Weight, ScaleNotes, Constraints.
- Users answer on a 1–5 scale (integer).
- NormScore = (response - 1) / 4 mapping 1..5 to 0..1.
- WeightedPts = NormScore * Weight.
- OverallScore = 100 * SUM(WeightedPts) across all 20 questions.
- OverallScore must be blank/undefined until ALL required questions have answers.
- OverallRating bands come from sheet “Config”:
  0–54.999 = High Risk
  55–69.999 = Elevated Risk
  70–84.999 = Moderate Risk
  85–100 = Strong / Low Risk
- Pillar scores:
  Safety is sum of WeightedPts where Pillar=Safety divided by pillar weight share (0.4), multiplied by 100.
  WorkersComp similarly using weight share (0.2), multiplied by 100.
  Fleet similarly using weight share (0.4), multiplied by 100.

SUBCONTRACTOR SUB-SCORE: Create a separate mini-form based on sheet “Subcontractor_SubScore” with 3 controls:

Compute SubcontractorWeightedAvg = SUM(score*weight) (range 1–5).
